## One more game

PONGIT is a head-to-head Pong arcade on Monad Testnet. Move your paddle, find a rival and race to seven points. The browser draws the action; smart contracts determine collisions, scores and the winner.

<Callout title="Testnet arcade">PONGIT currently uses test MON on chain 10143. An ordinary match awards ranking points, not a MON prize. Playing is gas-sponsored while the platform has funding.</Callout>

## Choose your night

- **Play now** enters the ranked Classic queue by default. [Start your first match](/docs/getting-started/first-match).
- **Chaos** adds crowd pressure: betting on the favourite can shrink that player's paddle in the next rally. [Read the rules](/docs/playing/chaos).
- **Challenge a friend** lets you pick an opponent. Friendly games leave both rankings unchanged.
- **Watch live** opens current matches. Spectators can follow the score without connecting a wallet.
- **More** contains the leaderboard, tournaments and replays.

## Your place in the arcade

A [passkey](/docs/getting-started/passkeys) creates your Mera account without a browser extension. You can add a public username and character, keep a private encrypted notebook and return to the same account later.

The active deployment is V4. Its winning bets and tournament prizes are paid directly to the beneficiary's wallet. Older balances stay in their original vaults and have a separate withdrawal flow.

## What is available today

Classic, Chaos, direct challenges, rankings, tournaments, live spectators, retained replays, public profiles, the private notebook and automatic V4 payments are available. Interlude is **not integrated** and remains paused. [See how the system works](/docs/technical/architecture).

The guides describe the delivered application. Where the network, indexer or sponsorship is delayed, the interface reports the operation's state rather than promising instant finality.
