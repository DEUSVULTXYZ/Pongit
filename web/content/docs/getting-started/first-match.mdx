## Enter the arcade

On your first visit, choose **Enter arcade** for audio or **Enter muted**. The choice is remembered; you can change it in **Arcade settings**. Classic is the initial mode, with **Ranked · Free to play** shown under the main button.

1. Select **Play now**.
2. Create a passkey, or continue with the account you already use.
3. Approve the limited arcade session when requested. PONGIT continues into matchmaking automatically.
4. Wait for an opponent and the onchain match to start.

You do not need a public profile before playing. Cancelling the connection abandons that Play now request. If you already have an active match, use **Resume match**.

## Move your paddle

Use **W / S** or **↑ / ↓**. On a touch screen, hold the up/down controls beneath the court. Release to stop. The match header shows whether you control the left or right paddle.

The first player to score **seven points** wins. The solid paddle previews your movement immediately; a score or collision becomes authoritative when confirmed by the contract. [Understand the confirmation delay](/docs/technical/physics).

## During the match

**Cabinet tools** contains session actions and **Concede**. Opening an overlay or losing focus clears held controls. Conceding ends your participation with a loss; it is different from cancelling a queue search before a match exists.

If your session expires, use **Reconnect** to renew it. Do not create a new passkey to recover an existing player.

## After the result

The confirmed result triggers a skippable four-second **VICTORY** or **DEFEAT** sequence. Then choose **Rematch**, **Watch replay** or **Find another opponent**. Rematch invites the player you just faced; they must accept. Ranked games update the appropriate ELO ladder. Ordinary victories do not pay MON.
