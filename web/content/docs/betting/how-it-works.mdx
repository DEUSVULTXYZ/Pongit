## Open the match market

Join a Chaos room as a spectator and open **Market** in the top bar. Connect your passkey to use the market. Classic rooms do not open betting markets; the V4 arcade retains its own markets. Choose which player to back and the number of shares. Purchases use the active **betting balance**, not an automatic debit from incoming wallet winnings.

A winning share pays **1 MON per whole share**; fractional shares pay proportionally. The displayed potential payout is the gross payment if that side wins, not profit in addition to your purchase cost. Losing shares pay zero.

## Review before signing

The review shows the selected player, **Estimated cost**, **Signed maximum** and **Payout if this player wins**. In rooms, the maximum adds a 3% tolerance and conservative rounding to the displayed quote. The V4 review retains its 1% tolerance. The signed maximum bounds the spend. Quotes are state-dependent and can become stale while your passkey prompt is open.

Select **Confirm bet with passkey** only after reviewing the current details. If the market state changes beyond the permitted amount or closes, refresh the quote and review again. A gameplay key has no permission to approve a bet.

The account panel offers **Get test betting credit** and **Withdraw credit to wallet**, both with owner approval. Wallet winnings are not automatically deposited as betting credit.

## Why prices move

PONGIT uses a binary **LMSR** market maker. Buying shares changes the cost of subsequent purchases according to the outstanding shares and configured liquidity. The platform deposits real test-MON liquidity; the contract checks reserves against possible payouts. The current interface supports buying and settlement, not selling shares back before the result.

## When bets are unavailable

- A participant's account cannot bet on its own match.
- The market must be funded, opened and unpaused, and the match must be in play.
- In rooms, bets are accepted only during the 40-block window opened after a published Chaos point. No bet can enter a rally retroactively. The V4 market retains its collision guard.
- Quotes bind the game state; an outdated version can be rejected.

These restrictions reduce latency advantages, but do not guarantee their elimination or identify every related wallet.

## Chaos changes the game

<Callout title="Backing a player can make their next rally harder">Chaos counts accepted MON costs toward crowd pressure. Beyond the threshold, stronger support shrinks the favourite's paddle at the next rally. Read the warning before signing.</Callout>
