## Quick answers

### Is playing free?

Gameplay gas is sponsored while the relayer is funded. Bets and fee-bearing tournament entries use test credits. Disabling a daily sponsorship ceiling does not make funding unlimited.

### Do I get MON for every win?

No. Ordinary ranked games award ELO; friendly games award neither ELO changes nor a new MON prize. Winning bets and tournament prizes have their own accounting.

### Do I need a wallet extension?

No. PONGIT uses a Mera passkey account. The provider must support PRF. Your public username is not your wallet or recovery credential.

### Can I bet on myself or sell a position?

Participant accounts cannot bet on their own match. The current LMSR interface supports buying and settlement, not early resale.

### Why are old claim buttons different?

V4 pays due amounts directly to the wallet. V1–V3 credit their old vaults and still require a signed withdrawal. [Balances](/docs/account/balances).

### Is Interlude live?

No. The active transport is Monad. Interlude remains paused; no advertised low-latency target should be treated as a measured PONGIT result.

### Can I recover every replay?

The app retains the latest three eligible finished games per participant. Events remain onchain, but the site does not automatically rebuild retired replays.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning in PONGIT |
| --- | --- |
| MON | The chain's native asset; this application currently uses test MON |
| Gas | Transaction execution cost, paid by the sponsor for supported gameplay |
| Passkey / PRF | Credential and deterministic key material used by Mera |
| Arcade session | A limited, expiring gameplay authorization |
| ELO | A rating based on ranked results and expected win probability |
| Rally | Play between two points; Chaos sizes stay fixed for that rally |
| Vault | Contract holding available betting credit |
| Share | A position paying proportionally if its side wins |
| LMSR | The binary market's cost function |
| Relayer | Service submitting supported signed actions and maintenance transactions |
| Envio | Indexer turning confirmed contract events into queryable records |
| Snapshot | An observed game state used for display and reconstruction |
| Versioned reference | Deployment plus match ID, such as `v4:12` |
| Settled result | A confirmed authoritative outcome, not just a rendered preview |
