## Open a live arena

Use **Watch live** on the home cabinet or **Live** in the navigation. Select a current match to follow its score and court. You do not need a passkey just to watch.

The match header labels the view as **SPECTATOR VIEW**. It shows the mode and whether the match is ranked or friendly. If a player has a public username, that name replaces the shortened address in the scoreboard.

## Read the action

Spectators receive network snapshots and see a rendered preview between confirmations. The contracts still determine impacts and points. **LIVE PREVIEW**, **AWAITING IMPACT CONFIRMATION** or **SYNCING / PREVIEW PAUSED** describe the display's relationship to confirmed state.

In Chaos, the rally banner shows each paddle's size and whether the match is in intermission. A betting-induced handicap changes the next rally, not the current collision.

At a confirmed result, spectators see a neutral winner announcement rather than a personal VICTORY or DEFEAT celebration.

## Support a player

Eligible spectators can connect a passkey and use the match's market. Review the cost and potential payout before approving any test-MON spend. A participant's account cannot bet on its own game. [Learn about betting](/docs/betting/how-it-works).

## When the feed pauses

An empty Live list means there are no listed active matches. If the feed disconnects, wait for reconnection and a fresh snapshot. The visual pause does not establish that the onchain game paused. After completion, the result remains available; a replay is available only while it satisfies [the retention policy](/docs/playing/replays).
