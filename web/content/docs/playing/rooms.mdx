## Three ways to play

**Matchmaking** finds a ranked opponent in the selected **Classic** or **Chaos** mode. The queues are separate. **Invite someone** opens contacts and username or wallet-address search for a friendly duel. **Create room** opens a shared cabinet with up to eight people, including the two current players. Connect once with your existing Mera passkey. The chosen action continues after connection or renewal. An unexpired Interlude session is restored after F5 in the same tab.

Each deployment has its own game authorization. Your wallet address stays the same. A rooms grant lasts 30 minutes and can accept a match, move a paddle, advance its physics, cancel a pending match or concede. It cannot spend betting credit, withdraw MON or administer the contracts. The wallet key is closed after authorization. **Disconnect** removes this tab's game key; it does not revoke copies on other devices.

## Accept a duel

A targeted invitation is reserved for its recipient and expires after ten minutes. A group-room link requests one of the available seats. The invitation shows the identity and **Accept / Back**. Accepting a room invitation also accepts its first proposed duel when available.

Every pairing requires both players' approval of the same signed ticket within twenty seconds. **Back**, or failing to respond, marks a player away. **Rejoin queue** places them at the end of the room queue. A submitted acceptance is checked on the engine before cancellation. The coordinator cannot invent a score or choose a winner.

## Share a cabinet

Two members play while the others automatically see the match. After a verified result, the winner stays and the loser moves to the end of the queue. The next available member is proposed. With two members, both can accept another game. Leaving the room transfers the host role to the longest-present member. A connection loss is not a concession.

During a match, **Members** opens the room list and **Tools** contains network status and **Concede**. **Copy address** and **Copy room link** provide a brief confirmation or a manual copy field when the clipboard is unavailable. The court remains rectangular, with the score and touch controls visible. Technical panels and financial forms do not sit below it.

A player can occupy one room or matchmaking queue at a time. Empty rooms expire after thirty minutes; inactive rooms expire after twenty-four hours. Initially, two matches can run concurrently. Other rooms remain open and display **Waiting for an arena** while publication capacity is unavailable.

## Contacts and recent rivals

Save or remove a contact by username or address. This is a private, server-stored address book without friendship requests. It is separate from the encrypted Mera notebook. Suggested rivals are the eight most frequent opponents in the last thirty days, using verified Interlude and V1 to V4 results, with the most recent encounter breaking ties. Existing invitation blocks also apply in rooms.

## Rules, rankings and old balances

Games remain first to seven. Each successful paddle return increases ball velocity by ten percent, without a gameplay speed cap; every point resets the next serve. Walls do not increase speed.

Classic carries forward the previous rooms ELO. Chaos starts a separate rating at 1000. The header has one **Ranking** entry with a mode selector. The contract applies placements, diminishing gains for repeated pairings and one rating update per result. Rooms and direct invitations are friendly and leave ELO unchanged. Live ELO and its published Monad copy are shown separately. Publication remains subject to Interlude's challenge process.

Chaos rooms offer between-rally prediction markets to spectators through **Market**. They use a separate vault, and payouts wait for delegation finality. Ordinary wins never award a MON prize. **V4 arcade** retains its original Chaos, tournaments, markets, payouts, notebook, rankings and replays. Existing vaults are not migrated, and old match links continue to refer to their original contracts. Rooms retain result summaries, not an unlimited recording of gameplay frames.

## Betting and maintenance

The rooms account opens **Betting credit & wallet** for credits, signed withdrawals and recent positions. In Chaos, the testnet VPS bridge attests the MON actually paid at a fixed cutoff. Each window is 40 Monad blocks, approximately twelve seconds, plus publication and confirmation delays. The game waits at that boundary. [Chaos rules](/docs/playing/chaos).

New games pause during scheduled delegation renewal. Payouts wait until the delegation closes and the one-hour challenge period has elapsed. They then go to the fixed wallet automatically, even if the recipient is disconnected. [Payment states](/docs/betting/payments).
