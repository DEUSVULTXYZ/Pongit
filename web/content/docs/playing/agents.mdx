## Choose your opponent

**Play a person** keeps human matchmaking separate. A bot never silently replaces a person. **Play an agent** opens the catalogue; **Watch agents** opens live matches. Invitations and rooms remain separate choices.

**PONGIT BOT** is an official identity verified by the contract registry. A similar username or avatar does not confer that status. **ON-CHAIN STRATEGY** is a community developer's registered contract. Its creator, modes, qualification and availability are public. Externally hosted community controllers are disabled in this release.

Controllers use public game state and cannot read future Chaos draws. They do not call a language model for each movement. Tournament learning follows the tournament rather than resetting whenever an arena renews its delegation.

## Play an agent

Choose **Classic** or **Chaos**, then an agent's challenge button, such as **Challenge NOVA**. Connection preserves this choice. The first approval authorizes the agent arena family for two hours without changing your Mera address or granting spending permissions.

Submitting a challenge expresses your agreement to that friendly match. The strategy supplies the other participant; there is no second two-human acceptance screen. A busy agent finishes its current participation first. A tournament participant becomes available after leaving that tournament. **Cancel challenge** removes a waiting request. **Concede match**, in **Tools**, ends a started game.

If no suitable arena is ready, the request waits. Agent traffic never borrows a human arena. F5 in the same tab restores an unexpired limited session and reconciles pending commands. A connection failure alone should not require another passkey ceremony.

## Match rules

A game ends at seven points or after five minutes of contract-measured time. The higher score wins at the time limit. Equal scores produce a draw, except for the elimination tie-break below. A Chaos double point can finish a game, with the displayed score capped at seven.

Classic retains human-game physics. Chaos includes the [24 arcade events](/docs/playing/chaos), verified randomness and pixel rendering. There are no bets, entry fees or prizes in Agent Arcade. Financial handicap pressure is neutral and cannot be changed through these contracts.

Human challenges are friendly and never change human ELO. Agents have separate Classic and Chaos ratings starting at 1000. Matches between declared agents of the same creator do not change ELO, but their results count in the tournament. A draw or administrative advancement never becomes a fabricated win.

## Automatic tournaments

**Tournaments** shows the bracket or standings, current match, upcoming fixtures and previous results. The order is elimination Classic, elimination Chaos, championship Classic, championship Chaos, with a one-minute interval after completion.

Each tournament selects eight available, qualified participants. Those who waited longest since their previous tournament have priority; addresses break ties. Official bots fill available places. Contracts lock participants across both modes and freeze strategy versions. A challenge cannot interrupt a fixture or take an agent out of its tournament.

**Elimination** has quarter-finals, semi-finals and a final. A tie after five minutes enters sudden death for at most one minute. If neither player scores, the higher initial ELO advances; equal ELO uses address order. This is labelled administrative advancement and gives no ELO.

**Championship** has 28 fixtures, one per pair. A win gives three table points, a draw one, a defeat zero. Ties use score difference, then wins, initial ELO and address order.

Progression uses results published on Monad. Those results can still be contested. A correction recalculates championship standings or invalidates dependent elimination branches before they resume. Earlier attempts and their complete contract references remain visible.

## Watch and replay

Watching needs no wallet. The arena distinguishes live engine state, a result awaiting publication, a published result still contestable, and a final result. **Copy arena link** copies the complete versioned match address.

**Watch replay** opens recorded contract snapshots with playback and a position slider. It does not reconnect to the old engine. A recording with gaps is labelled **PARTIAL REPLAY**. An old link never attaches to a replacement match in a renewed arena.

The shared history policy retains a replay while it belongs to either participant's three latest completed games. A retired replay keeps its compact result. Missing indexing is shown as synchronization; a changed published result makes the old recording unavailable. See [Replays & match history](/docs/playing/replays).

## Availability and recovery

Several games inside one delegation reduce session turnover. They do not remove the protocol's exit delay. Once a delegation closes, its arena cannot host another session until release and renewal are verified. New games need a different ready arena during that interval.

The private candidate has demonstrated consecutive games and renewal, but two configured arenas alone have not demonstrated continuous availability. Public opening requires measured reserve capacity and a complete endurance trial. A replay or an idle container never counts as an available live match.

Human funds and historical withdrawals remain outside the agent deployment. See the [developer protocol](/docs/technical/agent-sdk) for registration, authorization and recovery details.
