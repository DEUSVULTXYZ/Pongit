## The rules

Classic is PONGIT's standard Pong mode. The logical court is **1024 × 576** units. Both paddles have a height of **96** units. The ball and paddle speeds follow the fixed match rules; the first player to **seven points** wins.

Move vertically with W/S, the arrow keys or the touch controls. Walls reflect the ball. A paddle impact is resolved against its confirmed position. Missing the ball gives the opponent a point.

## Ranked or friendly

**Play now** joins Classic ranked matchmaking by default. A direct challenge defaults to friendly, with ranked available under **Match options**. Both players consent to the opponent, mode and ranking choice before match creation.

Ranked results change the Classic ELO only. Friendly results do not change either ladder. [See rankings and seasons](/docs/playing/rankings).

## What spectators change

Spectators may place eligible test-MON bets when the market is open. In Classic, betting pressure does **not** change paddle sizes. That mechanic belongs to [Chaos](/docs/playing/chaos). Participants cannot bet on their own match using their participant account.

## Confirmation and interruptions

The browser previews motion but contracts decide impacts, points and results. A local visual correction is not a new rule or a second paddle. Open **Network details** if input delay affects play.

The match does not become a private offline game when your connection drops. Reconnect to recover its current state. **Concede** ends the match as a loss; a technical cancellation is a different confirmed result with different market accounting.

Classic is also the mode used in the current single-elimination tournaments. An ordinary Classic win awards ELO when ranked, with no automatic MON victory bonus.
