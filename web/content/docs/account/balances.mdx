## Two balances, two purposes

Open the account button in the header. The account panel shows the full address, an explorer link and separate balances.

| Balance | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Wallet** | Native test MON held by your Mera account; V4 winnings arrive here |
| **Betting balance** | Available credit held in the active vault for authorized bets and tournament entry fees |
| **Legacy vaults** | Separate balances belonging to V1, V2 and V3 |

Receiving a prize in your wallet does not automatically put it back into betting credit. There is no automatic reinvestment.

## Get test credits

Use **Get test credits** from the account panel. The application verifies an owner-signed request and applies its funding and abuse limits. Credits are for this testnet application, not mainnet currency. The daily sponsorship ceiling is disabled, but relayer funding, transaction limits and rate limits still apply.

If funding is unavailable, the interface reports it. Repeated clicking cannot create funds; wait for the service to recover.

## Withdraw available credit

Expand **Withdraw test MON**, enter the recipient and an amount within the available betting balance, then select **Sign withdrawal** and approve with your owner passkey. The arcade session cannot authorize a withdrawal. Wait for the actual transaction confirmation before treating the wallet balance as updated.

## Older vaults and claims

Expand **Older balances** to access V1–V3 separately. Their eligible claims or refunds credit that version's vault, followed by a **signed withdrawal** to your wallet. They do not gain V4's direct-wallet behaviour retroactively.

Always check the selected version and its balance. No migration automatically moves old funds or combines four vaults into one. A retired replay does not remove the claim or withdrawal right.

For new V4 positions, [automatic payouts](/docs/betting/payments) replace the old claim workflow. An ordinary match does not create a MON prize for its winner.
