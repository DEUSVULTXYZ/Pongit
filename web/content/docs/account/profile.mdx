## Put your name on the cabinet

From home, select **Create your profile** beneath the cabinet, or **Edit your profile** if you already have one. Connecting or renewing continues into the editor without starting matchmaking. The same editor is under **Rivals → Your public profile**.

Your profile is optional. Playing without one displays your shortened wallet address.

## Pick a unique username

Use **3–20 characters**, starting with a letter. Letters, digits and underscores are accepted; names are normalized to lowercase. `Neon_Rider` and `neon_rider` are therefore the same name.

The availability message is a preview, not a reservation. **Save public profile** performs the authoritative uniqueness check. If someone saves that name first, choose a different one. Saving requires authentication as the connected account.

## Choose your character

The roster contains **Nova, Ghost, Vector, Pulse, Drift, Kitsune, Ion, Echo, Onyx, Glitch, Solar and Viper**. Select a portrait to update the large preview, then save. Character names are shared presets; your username is what must be unique.

You can use arrow keys within the roster, Home/End for its first/last character, and Tab to leave. The selected border and checkmark identify your choice. There is no image-upload feature in this release.

## Where your profile appears

Your saved username and avatar follow you into home, Rivals and the leaderboard. The scoreboard displays the username, while the linked wallet address remains verifiable. F5 reloads the saved profile from the server.

**Remove my public profile** removes the public tag, not your passkey, results, ELO or funds. Public names and avatars are separate from [private notebook nicknames and notes](/docs/account/notebook). Unsaved changes are not a new published profile.
