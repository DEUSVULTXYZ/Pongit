## Put your name on the cabinet

Connect from the header, then open the account button to reach **Your account**. Choose a username and avatar, then **Save profile**. Successful confirmation closes the editor. The preserved V4 arcade has its original profile editor.

Your profile is optional. Playing without one displays your shortened wallet address.

## Pick a unique username

Use **3–20 characters**, starting with a letter. Letters, digits and underscores are accepted; names are normalized to lowercase. `Neon_Rider` and `neon_rider` are therefore the same name.

**Save profile** performs the contract's authoritative uniqueness check and requests the owner's signature. If someone saves that name first, choose a different one. Names from the migration snapshot are reserved for their original wallet owners; saving claims that reservation. A public name never changes the address derived from your passkey.

## Choose your character

The roster contains **Nova, Ghost, Vector, Pulse, Drift, Kitsune, Ion, Echo, Onyx, Glitch, Solar and Viper**. Select a portrait to update the large preview, then save. Character names are shared presets; your username is what must be unique.

You can use arrow keys within the roster, Home/End for its first/last character, and Tab to leave. The selected border and checkmark identify your choice. There is no image-upload feature in this release.

## Where your profile appears

Your saved username and avatar appear in invitations, the scoreboard and the leaderboard. F5 reloads the profile from its Monad registry. Until a reserved profile is claimed, the account editor offers its previous name and avatar for saving.

Public names and avatars are separate from [private notebook nicknames and notes](/docs/account/notebook). Unsaved changes are not a published profile. Historical registry transactions remain public even after a later edit.
